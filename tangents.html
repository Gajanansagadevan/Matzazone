G.C.E Ordinary Level Mathematical Theorems

Tangents

Theorem1: If two tangents are drawn to a circle from an external point, then,
  • (i) the two tangents are equal in length.
  • (ii) the angle between the tangents is bisected by the straight line joining the external point to the centre.
  • (iii) the tangents subtend equal angles at the centre.
    Theorem: The angles which a tangent to a circle makes with a chord drawn from the point of contact are respectively equal to the angles in the alternate segments of the circle.
