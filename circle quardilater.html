G.C.E Ordinary Level Mathematical Theorems

Circle quardilater

Theorem 1: The opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary.
Theorem 2: If the opposite angles of a quadrilateral are supplementary, then the vertices of the quadrilateral are on the circle.
Theorem 3: If one side of a cyclic quadrilateral is produced, the exterior angle so formed is equal to the interior opposite angle of the quadrilateral.
Theorem 4: The straight line drawn through a point on a circle and perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact, is a tangent to the circle.
Converse of the theorem
 The tangent through a point on a circle is perpendicular to the radius drawn to the point of contact.
